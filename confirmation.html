What happens next

These steps are described, not built. A working studio would run them through its email and a third-party booking service.

  1. A reply within two business days, confirming the date and answering any questions.
  2. A 30-minute call to talk through your day, your venue, and what matters most to you.
  3. A proposal, contract, and deposit, signed and paid online through a client-management service. Your date is held once the deposit clears.
  4. Planning: a timeline built together about a month before the wedding.
