Accessibility statement

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA on every page.

What that means in practice

  • Every photograph has a text description of the moment and the people in it. Purely decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Text over the home page photograph sits on a dark overlay so it meets the 4.5:1 contrast ratio against any part of the image.
  • Nothing moves on its own. There is no autoplay carousel or video, and any animation respects your reduced-motion setting.
  • Every control works by keyboard and shows a visible focus indicator. Interactive targets are at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels.
  • The portfolio lightbox is a modal dialog: focus moves in, stays in, Escape closes it, and focus returns to where you were.
  • Forms have visible labels, mark required fields in text, and list any errors at the top with links to the fields.
  • The date checker and cost calculator announce their results through a live region without moving your focus.
  • Pages have one main heading, a logical heading order, landmark regions, and a skip link.
  • Pages work at 320 CSS pixels wide and at 200 percent text size without horizontal scrolling.

How we test

Every page is checked with axe DevTools and by a keyboard-only pass. Screen reader testing with NVDA in Firefox and VoiceOver in Safari on iOS, plus a zoom and reflow check, is scheduled for after the hackathon build.

Known issues

  • Photographs are currently placeholders while licensed images are sourced. Their text descriptions describe the intended photograph, not the placeholder.
  • Screen reader testing has not yet been completed. Findings will be listed here.

Tell us about a problem

If anything on this site is hard to use, email hello@iveeleague.example with the page address and what happened. You'll get a reply within two business days. Please note this is a demo site, so the address is fictional too.
