About Steven

I'm Steven Walker Junior. I've photographed weddings in and around Cambridge, Massachusetts since 2014, and nothing else since 2017.

Before that I spent six years as a newspaper photographer, which is where the habits come from: arrive early, learn the room, don't interrupt, and never miss the moment because you were fixing the last one. A wedding is a long news story where everyone is on your side.

The approach

Most of the day I'm documentary. I don't pose the ceremony, the toasts, or the dancing. For the forty minutes of portraits we schedule together, I give light direction and then wait for the real thing to happen in between.

I work alone unless you add a second photographer. One person is quieter, and a timeline built with me will always have time for the photographs that matter to you.

What you get

  • A planning call and a walk-through of the timeline about two months out.
  • Full coverage for your package hours, with no cap on the number of photographs.
  • A preview within a week and the full online gallery within six weeks.
  • A print release, so you can print wherever you like.

Where I work

Cambridge and Boston first, then Greater Boston, the North and South Shores, Cape Cod and the Islands, the Berkshires, southern New Hampshire, and Rhode Island. Weddings farther away are welcome; travel is quoted separately. See the cost calculator for the travel bands.
